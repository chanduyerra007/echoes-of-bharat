A LIVING LANDMARK

Heritage made by a community.

Jagannath Temple is not only historic architecture. It is part of ongoing worship, craft, food traditions, music and the annual Rath Yatra. Respect for living traditions is central to understanding the site.

12TH CENTURY · KALINGA STYLE

A temple city takes form

The present temple complex is generally associated with the 12th-century Eastern Ganga period. Its towering form belongs to the distinctive Kalinga architectural tradition of Odisha.

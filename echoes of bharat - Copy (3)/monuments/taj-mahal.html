1631 · A MEMORY BEGINS

A royal commission

After Mumtaz Mahal's death, Shah Jahan commissioned a memorial complex on the Yamuna riverfront. The project brought together designers, stonecutters, calligraphers and inlay artists.

ARCHITECT'S LENS

Geometry makes calm

The complex uses a strong central axis and balanced forms. The famous dome, minarets, platform and garden feel composed because every element is part of a larger visual rhythm.

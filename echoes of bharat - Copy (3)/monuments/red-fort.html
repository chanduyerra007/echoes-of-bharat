A FORT THAT WAS A CITY

Walls protect a world inside.

Built for Shah Jahan's new capital, the Red Fort contained halls of audience, private rooms, water channels, gardens and working spaces. Its architecture reveals how power and everyday life were arranged together.

1639 · A NEW CAPITAL

Shahjahanabad begins

Shah Jahan established a new capital in Delhi. The Red Fort was planned as its royal centre, linked to the city through gates, markets and formal routes.

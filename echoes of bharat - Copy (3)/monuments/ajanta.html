c. 2ND CENTURY BCE · FIRST CAVES

Cutting into the hillside

The earliest activity at Ajanta began with caves shaped for Buddhist monastic life. Rock-cut construction demanded planning: each space was carved out rather than assembled from blocks.

Painting tells through gesture

Ajanta's paintings use faces, hands and body positions to guide a viewer through stories. The artists made colour and expression carry ideas across the cave walls.

13TH CENTURY · A COASTAL KINGDOM

A temple for Surya

Built under King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty, Konark was conceived as an extraordinary expression of devotion, power and artistic knowledge.

ARCHITECT'S LENS

The wheel as a clock

The carved wheels are one of Konark's most powerful symbols. They make time visible: a circular form that suggests motion, seasons and the daily journey of the sun.
